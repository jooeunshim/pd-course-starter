# Air Quality and Bioinformatics

A professional development course for science teachers

---

Welcome. This course invites you to investigate air quality in your own community alongside your students, and to connect what you find to human biology and health. You will work with low cost sensors, analyze the data you collect, and design classroom activities that fit your curriculum and your students.

The course is built around discussion. In each unit you will write your own thinking first, then read and respond to colleagues. Your experience in your own classroom is the material this course works with.

## How the course is organized

Each module contains short readings, videos, and an activity. Activities link to a discussion thread where the whole cohort writes together. There are no quizzes and no grades. You set your own pace.
