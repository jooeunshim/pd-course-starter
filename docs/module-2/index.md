# Module 2. Science Education for Society

This module asks you to step back from the day to day of teaching and name what you think science education is for. We start with your own beliefs, then look at two ways scholars have described the purpose of scientific literacy, then return to your classroom.

## Units in this module

| Unit | Title | What you will do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2.1.1 | [Your beliefs about science education](beliefs.md) | Write about your own beliefs, respond to colleagues, watch two short videos |
| 2.1.2 | Vision 1 and Vision 2 in practice | Examine classroom examples and sort them *(available soon)* |
| 2.1.3 | Your curriculum, your context | Map one of your existing units onto the two visions *(available soon)* |

## Time

About 90 minutes, which you can spread across the week. The discussion stays open, so you can return to it after others have posted.
